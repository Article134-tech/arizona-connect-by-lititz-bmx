### Holbrook / Navajo County Fairgrounds

Recovered period Tribune-News evidence through the Arizona Memory Project documenting:

- 1989 newly completed BMX facility and opening-era racing at the Navajo County Fairgrounds;
- 1991 BMXpress track naming at the county fairgrounds;
- 1992 Painted Desert BMX Track naming at the same county-fairgrounds property.

Current official fairgrounds address and a USGS-referenced fairgrounds coordinate provide the property-level cross-check.

Disposition: historical site record admitted with SITE_POINT_APPROXIMATE. Exact historical track-center and formal name-transition dates remain open.
