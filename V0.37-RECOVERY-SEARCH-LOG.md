## Evidentiary discipline

Absence of “Mile-Hi” from the Prescott Valley historical-clipping compilation is **not** treated as evidence of absence. A later municipal BMX proposal cannot identify an earlier course. A generic planning reference to “a BMX track” cannot create a governed named-track identity. Municipal engineering plans around the Reid bandshell cannot be converted into a BMX footprint unless the course itself is shown or independently registered to that geometry.
