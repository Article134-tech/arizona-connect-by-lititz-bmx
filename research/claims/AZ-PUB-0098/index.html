PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Period Newspaper

AZ-PUB-0098

A 1989 Tribune-News report preserved in the Arizona Memory Project describes a newly completed BMX facility at the Navajo County Fairgrounds in Holbrook and reports the first races at the new facility.

Evidence typeFACT — PERIOD LOCAL NEWSPAPER / ARCHIVAL REPRODUCTION
Source classB1
ConfidenceHIGH FOR NEW 1989 FAIRGROUNDS BMX FACILITY

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

This establishes a newly completed BMX track at the Navajo County Fairgrounds in Holbrook in 1989. The archival newspaper evidence supports the property-level site and opening-era racing, but not an exact track-center coordinate within the fairgrounds or a formal later rename date.
