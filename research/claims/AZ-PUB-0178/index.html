PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Rebuild History

AZ-PUB-0178

A January 31, 2018 RiverScene report states that SARA Park BMX reopened on a newly rebuilt track after nearly two months of work and several years of fundraising by the TRI State BMX Association and SARA Park BMX crew.

Evidence typeFACT — CONTEMPORANEOUS LOCAL REPORT
Source classC1 local contemporary report
ConfidenceHIGH FOR REPORTED REBUILD ATTRIBUTION

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

This supports Tri-State's reported role in fundraising for the rebuild and the existence of the rebuilt SARA Park BMX track. It does not establish Tri-State ownership, exclusive operation, exact construction contracts, or legal succession to later Lake Havasu City BMX Association.
