PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Community History

AZ-PUB-0187

A March 2025 Copper Basin News obituary for longtime San Manuel resident Barbara Lynn Hagan states that Hagan ran Granny's Snack Shack at the Coppertown BMX track.

Evidence typeATTRIBUTED LOCAL PUBLISHED RETROSPECTIVE
Source classC2-local published retrospective
ConfidenceMEDIUM-HIGH FOR ATTRIBUTED STATEMENT

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

The obituary establishes a published retrospective attribution only. It does not date the snack shack's operation, establish Hagan as owner or operator of the BMX organization, identify the track's street address or footprint, establish sanction chronology, opening or closing dates, uninterrupted continuity, or justify a coordinate.
