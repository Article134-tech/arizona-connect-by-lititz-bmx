Growth 147–152 · Historical Tracks / Municipal Planning Context

AZ-PUB-0152

A Prescott Valley historical newspaper-clipping compilation records that on March 31, 1999 the Town Council declined a cost-free BMX-track proposal for Mountain Valley Park and suggested Antelope Park or a future Agua Fria Park as alternatives.

Evidence characterFACT — PERIOD NEWSPAPER TEXT PRESERVED BY MUNICIPAL HISTORICAL ARCHIVE
AuthorityB1 / A2 PRESERVATION
ConfidenceHIGH FOR REPORTED 1999 PROPOSAL DECISION AND ALTERNATIVE PARKS
AdmissionPASS

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

This is a 1999 planning/proposal record only. It does not locate 1983–1984 Mile-Hi BMX, establish that Mile-Hi was absent from Prescott Valley, identify the 1999 proposal with any prior or later track, or prove that any suggested alternative was built.
