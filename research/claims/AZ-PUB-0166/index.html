AZ-PUB-0166

A reproduced 1997 obituary for Diane Sandberg-Laipple says she moved with her parents Ray and Ruby Sandberg to Prescott Valley in 1976 to open a family restaurant, continued helping at Sandberg’s Family Restaurant, and identifies Scott Sandberg of Prescott Valley as her brother.

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

This independently documents a Scott Sandberg of Prescott Valley as a member of the Sandberg family associated with Sandberg’s Family Restaurant. It does not establish that this Scott Sandberg is the same person named as the 1984 Mile-Hi BMX contact, establish his ownership or employment at the restaurant, connect him to Scott’s Bike Shop, connect either business to Mile-Hi BMX, reverse 602-772-8050, or locate the track or Summer Camp footprint.
