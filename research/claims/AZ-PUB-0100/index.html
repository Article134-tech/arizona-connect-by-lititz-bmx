AZ-PUB-0100

A 1992 Tribune-News report preserved in the Arizona Memory Project identifies Painted Desert BMX Track at the Navajo County Fairgrounds in Holbrook and reports an April 4 opening day for the 1992 racing season.

Evidence characterFACT — PERIOD LOCAL NEWSPAPER / ARCHIVAL REPRODUCTION

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

This establishes the Painted Desert BMX Track name at the same county-fairgrounds property in 1992 and the April 4 season-opening race day. It does not establish the exact formal rename date from BMXpress, uninterrupted sanction continuity, or the precise track-center coordinate.
