AZ-PUB-0147

The City of Tucson preserves construction plan L-63-006, a scanned 1963 “Master Plan and Drainage Randolph Park” record with two surviving plan sheets.

Evidence characterFACT — OFFICIAL MUNICIPAL ENGINEERING ARCHIVE
AuthorityA1
ConfidenceHIGH FOR PLAN ID, DATE, DESCRIPTION AND SURVIVING SCANS

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

The 1963 master plan is a pre-band-stage, pre-BMX spatial-control record for Randolph Park. It does not label a BMX course and cannot locate the later 1977 Randolph Park BMX Track by itself. The Atlas uses it only to register earlier park geometry around the later recovery problem.
