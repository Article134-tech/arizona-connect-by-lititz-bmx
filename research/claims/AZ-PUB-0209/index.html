PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Operations

AZ-PUB-0209

A 1992 Tribune-News report says memberships were continuing to increase at Painted Desert BMX Track at the Navajo County Fairgrounds in Holbrook and that racing was held each Saturday through the end of October.

Evidence typeFACT — PERIOD LOCAL NEWSPAPER / OPERATING-SCHEDULE REPORT
Source classB1
ConfidenceHIGH FOR REPORTED 1992 MEMBERSHIP GROWTH + SATURDAY SCHEDULE

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

This establishes a period-reported operating cadence and membership-growth statement for Painted Desert BMX Track in 1992. It does not establish exact membership totals, uninterrupted operation beyond the reported season, or a formal relationship date between BMXpress and Painted Desert BMX Track.
