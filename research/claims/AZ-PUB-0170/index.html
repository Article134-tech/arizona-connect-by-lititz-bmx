AZ-PUB-0170

An October 30, 2018 Bullhead City local-news feature reports that the Colorado River BMX track off Highland Road reopened in August 2018 under Turnell Henry after having been closed for several years.

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

This supports the reported August 2018 reopening of the locally named Colorado River BMX track off Highland Road under Turnell Henry and a preceding multi-year closure. It does not establish the exact beginning of that closure, prove uninterrupted identity or legal continuity from the May 1999 nonprofit record, connect the track to the 1984–1985 Bullhead City Kiwanis BMX or Bullhead BMX identities, or establish that the 2018 course footprint was geometrically identical to the present track.
