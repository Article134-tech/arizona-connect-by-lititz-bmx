PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Current Tracks / Municipal Parks

AZ-PUB-0188

Yuma County's adopted 2020 Parks Master Plan identifies Yuma BMX Park as one of the County's two special-purpose parks and says the County relies on agreements with volunteers, community groups and other third parties to maintain and operate its parks.

Evidence typeFACT — COUNTY-ADOPTED PARKS MASTER PLAN
Source classA2
ConfidenceHIGH FOR COUNTY PARK CLASSIFICATION AND 2020 OPERATING MODEL

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

This establishes Yuma BMX Park as a Yuma County special-purpose park and documents the County’s general agreement-based operating model in the adopted 2020 plan. It does not identify the specific 2020 Yuma BMX lessee, reproduce the terms of any BMX agreement, or prove uninterrupted operation from an earlier period.
