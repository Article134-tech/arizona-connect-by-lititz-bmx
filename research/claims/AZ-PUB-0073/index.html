PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · People / Site History

AZ-PUB-0073

Lake Havasu City Parks and Recreation Advisory Board minutes state that pro BMX rider Corey Bohan visited Patrick Tinnell Memorial Sports Park in 2016 and that the visit was to be featured in Ride BMX Magazine.

Evidence typeFACT — OFFICIAL MUNICIPAL RECORD
Source classA2
ConfidenceHIGH

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

The city minutes establish the visit and the planned magazine feature as reported to the Board. They do not establish the final contents or publication date of the Ride BMX feature without the magazine itself.
