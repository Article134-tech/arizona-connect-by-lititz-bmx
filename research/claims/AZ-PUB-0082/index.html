PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Dated Directory

AZ-PUB-0082

The March 2002 Cycling Utah ABA track directory listed Sara Park BMX in Lake Havasu City, Arizona.

Evidence typeFACT — DATED REGIONAL BMX DIRECTORY
Source classC1
ConfidenceHIGH FOR DATED DIRECTORY LISTING

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

Together with the separate 1998 London Bridge BMX evidence, this narrows the historical name sequence in Lake Havasu City but does not prove that London Bridge BMX and Sara Park BMX were the same organization or physical track.
