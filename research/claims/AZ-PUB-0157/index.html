SUPPORTING EVIDENCE · Historical Tracks / Municipal Planning Context

AZ-PUB-0157

The Town of Chino Valley’s adopted 2014 General Plan states that the Town acquired the Old Home Manor property, comprising more than 800 acres, in 1979.

Supporting Evidence Record

Useful recovery, spatial, archival, family, civic or municipal context for an Arizona BMX investigation, but not itself a standalone Arizona BMX claim.

This record is preserved and source-linked, but it is not counted among the 173 public Arizona claims.

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

The 1979 acquisition establishes municipal property chronology for Old Home Manor. It does not show that a BMX track existed there in 1979, connect the property to the 1984 Chino Valley BMX directory identity, or prove succession from the 2002–2003 Mile High BMX record at Memory Park.
