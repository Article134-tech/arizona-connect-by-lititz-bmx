PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Community Construction

AZ-PUB-0205

A 1989 Tribune-News report says another 1,500 to 1,600 yards of fill dirt were needed to complete the Holbrook BMX track’s berms, curves and jumps, that more than 110 truck trips carried fill, and that National Bike League representative Bob Tedesco was in Holbrook to engineer the construction.

Evidence typeFACT — PERIOD LOCAL NEWSPAPER / CONSTRUCTION REPORT
Source classB1
ConfidenceHIGH FOR REPORTED CONSTRUCTION SCALE + NBL ENGINEERING ROLE

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

This preserves the period newspaper’s reported construction quantities, truck activity and Bob Tedesco/NBL engineering role. It does not reconstruct the final course geometry, establish a licensed engineer-of-record relationship beyond the wording of the report, or prove which individual features survived later changes.
