AZ-PUB-0165

An August 1, 1980 Prescott Valley fact sheet preserved in the Arizona Memory Project lists both “Sandberg’s Family Restaurant” and “Scott’s Bike Shop” among businesses within Prescott Valley.

Evidence characterFACT — MUNICIPAL BUSINESS FACT SHEET / ARCHIVAL COPY
AuthorityA2
ConfidenceHIGH FOR BUSINESS-NAME / PRESCOTT VALLEY LISTING
AdmissionPASS

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

This establishes that both business names were present in Prescott Valley by August 1980, providing locality context four years before the Mile-Hi directory lists Scott Sandberg. It does not establish ownership of Scott’s Bike Shop, identify Scott Sandberg as owner/operator of either business, connect either business to Mile-Hi BMX, reverse the published 602-772-8050 number, or locate the track or Summer Camp footprint.
