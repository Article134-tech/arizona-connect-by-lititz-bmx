PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Municipal Planning Context

AZ-PUB-0151

A January 2009 Chino Valley Extension / SR 169 corridor study identifies Town-owned Old Home Manor Park as an 880-acre recreation property whose listed amenities included a BMX track.

Evidence typeFACT — PUBLIC PLANNING / ENVIRONMENTAL STUDY
Source classA2
ConfidenceHIGH FOR 2009 DOCUMENTED PARK OWNERSHIP, SIZE AND BMX-TRACK AMENITY

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

The study documents an unnamed BMX track at Old Home Manor Park in 2009. It does not provide a sanctioned track identity or prove succession, relocation, rename or organizational continuity from 1984 Chino Valley BMX or 2002–2003 Mile High BMX at the Memory Park address. No new census identity or geographic point is created from this generic facility reference.
