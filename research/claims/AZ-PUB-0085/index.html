AZ-PUB-0085

RAM BMX is independently listed in Tucson, Arizona in both the September 1998 BMX track guide and the March 2002 Cycling Utah ABA track directory.

Evidence typeRECONSTRUCTION — TWO DATED DIRECTORY SNAPSHOTS
Source classB1 + C1
ConfidenceHIGH FOR REPEATED PERIOD LISTING

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

Repeated listings support the RAM BMX name and Tucson association across the two dated snapshots. They do not prove uninterrupted operation, exact site, or a relationship to Sports Park BMX, Tucson BMX, Desert Sunset BMX, or Manzanita BMX Raceway.
