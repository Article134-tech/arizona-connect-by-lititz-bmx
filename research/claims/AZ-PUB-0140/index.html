AZ-PUB-0140

In a National BMX Hall of Fame interview, ABA founder Merl Mennenga recalls another Tucson BMX track as a downhill track in a city park and says Louis Stockmeyer ran it.

Evidence characterATTRIBUTED RETROSPECTIVE ORAL HISTORY — INSTITUTIONAL INTERVIEW
AuthorityB2
ConfidenceHIGH FOR ATTRIBUTED MENNENGA STATEMENT; MEDIUM-HIGH FOR REID/RANDOLPH ASSOCIATION
AdmissionPASS

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

Mennenga’s published recollection establishes his statement about a Tucson city-park downhill BMX track and Louis Stockmeyer. The interview excerpt itself does not name Randolph or Reid Park. Association with the Reid Park record is therefore supported by the separate 1984 directory naming Louie Stockmeyer for Reid Park BMX, not treated as a standalone exact-site identification.
