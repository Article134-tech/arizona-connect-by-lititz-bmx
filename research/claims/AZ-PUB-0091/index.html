PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Site History

AZ-PUB-0091

A September 6, 2017 RiverScene feature locates SARA Park BMX Raceway at 7260 Sara Park Lane and reports that the track had been rebuilt from the ground up about two years earlier while its asphalt turns remained in place.

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

This supports a physical SARA Park BMX track at the reported address and continuity of that physical track through the reported circa-2015 rebuild. It does not prove London Bridge BMX organizational identity, legal continuity, an exact early-1990s opening date, or uninterrupted sanctioning history.
