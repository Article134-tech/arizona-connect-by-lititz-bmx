PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Tracks / Municipal History

AZ-PUB-0071

Lake Havasu City Parks and Recreation Advisory Board minutes record a unanimous March 23, 2015 approval to develop a separate Strider track next to the regular BMX track at SARA Park.

Evidence characterFACT — OFFICIAL MUNICIPAL ACTION

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

The minutes prove the 2015 advisory-board approval and location next to the regular BMX track at SARA Park. They do not by themselves prove the exact completion/opening date or establish continuity among London Bridge BMX, Sara Park BMX Raceway and the current Lake Havasu City BMX identity.
