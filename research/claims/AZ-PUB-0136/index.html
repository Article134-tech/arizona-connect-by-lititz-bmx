PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Period Directory Identity

AZ-PUB-0136

The May 1985 BMX Action Arizona track directory lists “Phoenix, Heritage Park BMX” with Kathy Scott and published telephone numbers 602-861-6070 and 602-578-5620. The recovered June 1985 BMX Plus! Arizona directory does not repeat Heritage Park BMX; it instead lists Mesa BMX with Kathy Zosky and Debbie Cotton.

Evidence characterFACT — TWO PERIOD BMX PUBLICATIONS / DIRECTORY CORRECTION
AuthorityB1
ConfidenceHIGH FOR MAY 1985 PHOENIX HERITAGE PARK BMX IDENTITY / HIGH FOR JUNE DIRECTORY NON-REPETITION
AdmissionCORRECTED

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

This corrects the v0.35 Prescott / Kathy Zosky / Debbie Cotton attribution. The recovered evidence establishes a May 1985 Heritage Park BMX directory identity under Phoenix only. It does not establish the exact physical site, ownership or operator role, opening or closing dates, sanction continuity, or a relationship to Desert Edge BMX, Roadrunner Raceway, or another Phoenix-area track.
