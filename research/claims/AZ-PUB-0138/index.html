PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Geographic Registration

AZ-PUB-0138

The previously published Prescott Heritage Park location at 34.6133572, -112.4404465 is withdrawn from the Arizona BMX map. Heritage Park BMX is now documented to Phoenix only because the corrected period evidence places the track identity under Phoenix and no defensible Phoenix site coordinate has been recovered.

Evidence typeCORRECTION — GEOGRAPHIC PIN WITHDRAWAL
Source classB1 / A1 / A2 / C1 REVIEW
ConfidenceHIGH FOR PIN WITHDRAWAL / OPEN FOR EXACT PHOENIX SITE

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

The withdrawn coordinate remains a valid reference for the current City of Prescott Heritage Park, but it is no longer a BMX coordinate. No replacement Phoenix point is published. The exact 1985 Heritage Park BMX site remains unresolved.
