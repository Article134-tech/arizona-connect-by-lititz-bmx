AZ-PUB-0154

The City of Tucson field-book index preserves multiple Randolph Park survey records in Township 14 South, Range 14 East, Section 16, including book 0174 “TOPOGRAPHY AND LAYOUT” and book 0677 pages 35–79 “TOPOG, X-SECTIONS, BM CIRCUITS.”

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

The field-book index identifies survey/topography records for Randolph Park but does not label BMX, expose a dated 1977 course plan, or establish where the dirt course sat inside the park. The books are recovery targets, not an exact-site result.
