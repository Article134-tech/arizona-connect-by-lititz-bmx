AZ-PUB-0172

A cross-source record supports same-site continuity between Chandler Jaycees BMX and today’s Chandler BMX: a BMX history timeline says Merl Mennenga started Chandler Jaycees in 1975 and that the track remained in the same location; a June 1987 BMX Plus! retrospective identifies Chandler Jaycees as the ABA’s first track; and current USA BMX places Chandler BMX at Frye and McQueen while describing it as the first BMX track the ABA opened for sanctioned racing.
