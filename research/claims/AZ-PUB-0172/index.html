PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Site History

AZ-PUB-0172

A cross-source record supports same-site continuity between Chandler Jaycees BMX and today’s Chandler BMX: a BMX history timeline says Merl Mennenga started Chandler Jaycees in 1975 and that the track remained in the same location; a June 1987 BMX Plus! retrospective identifies Chandler Jaycees as the ABA’s first track; and current USA BMX places Chandler BMX at Frye and McQueen while describing it as the first BMX track the ABA opened for sanctioned racing.

Evidence typeCROSS-SOURCE SYNTHESIS — SPECIALIST SITE CONTINUITY + PERIOD TRACK IDENTITY + CURRENT FIRST-PARTY SITE
Source classB2 + B1 + A1
ConfidenceMEDIUM-HIGH FOR SAME PHYSICAL SITE; EXACT HISTORICAL COURSE GEOMETRY OPEN

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

This supports Chandler Jaycees BMX and current Chandler BMX occupying the same physical track site at the Frye/McQueen ground. It does not establish the exact 1975 or 1979–1983 dirt-course center, identical course geometry through time, every operator or sanction transition, uninterrupted organizational identity under one legal entity, or an exact 1975 street-address formulation. The historical map marker represents the shared site approximately, while the current Chandler track location is documented exactly.
