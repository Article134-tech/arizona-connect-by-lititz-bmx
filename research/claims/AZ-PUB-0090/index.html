PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Conflict Control

AZ-PUB-0090

A 2013 IMCA points document separately lists “Thunder Raceway — Show Low, AZ” in an auto-racing competition context, while the 1998 BMX directory independently lists Thunder Raceway in Show Low as an ABA BMX track.

Evidence characterCONFLICT CONTROL — CROSS-SPORT SAME-NAME EVIDENCE
AuthorityB1 + B2
ConfidenceHIGH FOR NAME-CONFLICT CONTROL
AdmissionPASS

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

The two sources prove that the Thunder Raceway name was used in Show Low in BMX and later motor-racing contexts. They do not prove whether the facilities were geographically identical, related, renamed, or completely separate. No modern motor-racing coordinate may be assigned to the historical BMX record from this evidence.
