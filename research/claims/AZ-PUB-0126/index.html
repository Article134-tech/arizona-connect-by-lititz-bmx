PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Attributed Rider Memory

AZ-PUB-0126

In a 2015 memorial comment, Ken Barrier recalled meeting Ted Jorgensen in 1982 and racing the first night at Peoria Dunes before being invited to join Jorgensen’s team.

Evidence typePERSONAL TRUTH — ATTRIBUTED RETROSPECTIVE RIDER MEMORY
Source classC2
ConfidenceHIGH FOR ATTRIBUTED STATEMENT; MEDIUM FOR OPENING-ERA WITNESS

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

This preserves Barrier’s attributed personal recollection and supports an opening-era Peoria Dunes witness in 1982. It does not independently establish the exact opening date, sanction, track site, ownership, or that “first night” was the facility’s formal first-ever race.
