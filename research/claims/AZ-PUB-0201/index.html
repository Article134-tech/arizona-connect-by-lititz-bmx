PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Period Directory Continuity

AZ-PUB-0201

The March and April 1984 Bikes & Dirt Arizona directories both list Bullhead City Kiwanis with Kirk Jongsma and the same published telephone numbers, 602-758-7575 and 602-758-3002.

Evidence typeFACT — PERIOD BMX PUBLICATION / ADJACENT-DIRECTORY COMPARISON
Source classB1
ConfidenceHIGH FOR REPEATED MARCH–APRIL 1984 IDENTITY + CONTACT + NUMBERS

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

This establishes repeated adjacent-issue Bullhead City Kiwanis directory identity, contact and telephone numbers. It does not establish ownership, the exact historical track site, or formal continuity to the 1985 Bullhead identities or current Colorado River BMX.
