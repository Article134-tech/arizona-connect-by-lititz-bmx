PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Tracks / Municipal History

AZ-PUB-0061

Show Low City Council minutes state that the Show Low BMX track was originally constructed in 2010, hosted weekly races and sanctioned events for several years, and stopped holding official races after 2017 when revised sanctioning standards left the track out of compliance.

Evidence typeFACT — OFFICIAL MUNICIPAL RECORD
Source classA2
ConfidenceHIGH

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

The 2021 municipal retrospective supports the 2010 construction date, prior sanctioned activity and post-2017 official-race gap. It does not establish uninterrupted operation in every intervening year or prove continuity with the historical Thunder Raceway name.
