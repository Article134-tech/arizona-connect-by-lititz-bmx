PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Personal Truth

AZ-PUB-0139

A public 1977 photo caption identifies “Randolph Park BMX Track, Tucson AZ” and describes the pictured rider as nine years old on a Mongoose.

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

The public caption is preserved as attributed personal-history evidence for a Randolph Park BMX Track in Tucson in 1977. It is not an independently verified race record, does not identify the exact course footprint, and does not prove that every later Reid Park BMX reference describes one unchanged course.
