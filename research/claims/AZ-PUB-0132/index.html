PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Period Directory Identity

AZ-PUB-0132

The June 1985 BMX Plus! Arizona track directory prints “Window Rock, Window Rock BMX,” followed by “Window Rock BMX Indoor” and Jerry Pete’s contact line.

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

The directory page establishes Window Rock BMX Indoor as a printed period identity in the Window Rock listing. It does not establish whether this was a second facility, an indoor variant of Window Rock BMX, a rename, a shared organization, or the same physical site. The identities remain separate pending stronger evidence.
