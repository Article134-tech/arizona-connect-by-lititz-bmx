PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Period Publication

AZ-PUB-0143

The October 1983 issue of BMX Plus! says Brent and Brian Patterson were teaching BMX skills at the Mile-Hi BMX Summer Camp in Prescott and reports that each earned $1,000 per week.

Evidence typeFACT — PERIOD BMX PUBLICATION
Source classB1
ConfidenceHIGH FOR PUBLISHED INSTRUCTIONAL-ACTIVITY STATEMENT

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

This strengthens the 1983 Mile-Hi Summer Camp activity record and identifies the Patterson brothers as instructors in the period article. It does not identify the exact camp/track footprint, prove operator or ownership roles, or resolve why the 1983 article uses Prescott while the 1984 directories use Prescott Valley.
