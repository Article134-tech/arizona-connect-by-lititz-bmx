AZ-PUB-0131

The June 1985 BMX Plus! track directory lists Bullhead BMX in Bullhead City with John Schneider; its published telephone number matches the May 1985 Bullhead City Kiwanis BMX listing.

Evidence characterFACT — PERIOD BMX PUBLICATION / DIRECTORY COMPARISON
AuthorityB1

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

The matching published telephone number is a continuity signal across two differently named directory identities, not proof of a formal rename, legal or organizational succession, uninterrupted operation, or the same physical BMX site. Bullhead BMX is therefore preserved as a separate census identity.
