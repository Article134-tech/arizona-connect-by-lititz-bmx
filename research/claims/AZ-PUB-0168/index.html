PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Period Directory Identity

AZ-PUB-0168

The May 1985 BMX Action Arizona track directory lists “Phoenix, Desert Edge BMX” with Scott Koby and published telephone number 602-861-6070.

Evidence characterFACT — PERIOD BMX PUBLICATION / DIRECTORY
AuthorityB1
ConfidenceHIGH FOR MAY 1985 PUBLISHED PHOENIX IDENTITY; SITE OPEN
AdmissionPUBLIC_ADMISSION_READY

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

This establishes Desert Edge BMX as a separately named Phoenix historical track identity in the May 1985 period directory and preserves Scott Koby as the published contact. The same issue separately prints Heritage Park BMX with Kathy Scott and also includes 602-861-6070 among its numbers; that shared number is a relationship signal only. It does not prove the two names refer to the same facility, a rename, succession, shared ownership or operation, the same physical site, or uninterrupted activity. No exact Desert Edge address or course point is admitted.
