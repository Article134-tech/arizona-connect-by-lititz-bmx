Arizona State Parks’ Statewide Comprehensive Outdoor Recreation Plan appendices record a Tucson “Reid Park & Zoo Improvements” project in 1980 and a “Reid Regional Park Renovation” project in 1981.

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

These records establish documented park improvement and renovation activity shortly after the attributed 1977 Randolph Park BMX evidence. They do not identify the BMX course, state that the projects altered or removed it, establish a closure date, or support any coordinate change.
