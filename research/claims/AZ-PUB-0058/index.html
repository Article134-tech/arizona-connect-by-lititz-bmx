PUBLIC ARIZONA CLAIM · Historical Tracks / Municipal Record

AZ-PUB-0058

On June 16, 2003, the City of Tucson approved a lease with Desert Sunset BMX, Inc. for operation of a dirt bicycle track at the Harrison Landfill; USA BMX later listed Desert Sunset BMX in Tucson for Olympic Day on June 23, 2013.

Evidence typeRECONSTRUCTION — OFFICIAL MUNICIPAL RECORD + FIRST-PARTY BMX LISTING
Source classA2 + A1
ConfidenceHIGH

LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

These sources establish a 2003 Tucson lease for Desert Sunset BMX, Inc. at Harrison Landfill and a separate 2013 USA BMX track/event listing. They do not prove uninterrupted operation throughout the intervening decade or exact later lineage.
