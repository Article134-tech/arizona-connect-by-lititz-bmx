SUPPORTING EVIDENCE RECORD · Historical Tracks / Federal Record

AZ-PUB-0081

On March 10, 1998, the Federal Register identified Frank Sabatano as President of the London Bridge BMX Association and recorded his recommendation that bicycle helmets account for more serious accidents that could result from child bicycle racing or jumping.

Supporting Evidence Record

The Federal Register establishes London Bridge BMX Association and Frank Sabatano, but the source passage does not itself establish the Arizona/Lake Havasu nexus. Keep it as supporting evidence for the separately governed Lake Havasu investigation.

This record is preserved and source-linked, but it is not counted among the 151 public Arizona claims.

EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

This independently corroborates the London Bridge BMX Association identity and Sabatano's leadership in a BMX-racing context. It does not establish the track's exact Lake Havasu City site, opening or closing dates, or continuity to Sara Park BMX Raceway or current Lake Havasu City BMX.
