LIMITS OF THE EVIDENCE

This establishes the existence of near-period Tucson aerial holdings that can be used to test the Reid/Randolph bandshell-area hypothesis. No aerial from those sets has been admitted here as course-level evidence, so no BMX footprint or coordinate promotion follows.
