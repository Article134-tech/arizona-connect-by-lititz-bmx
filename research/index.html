v0.48 · CHANDLER JAYCEES SITE CONTINUITY RECOVERY 1

Chandler Jaycees now has a defensible same-site bridge — without pretending the historical course center is known.

March 1984 and May 1985 period directories repeat the exact Roadrunner Raceway name under Phoenix. The Atlas preserves the changed contacts/numbers and keeps the May 1982 “Road Runner in Phoenix,” Road Runner Bike Center, Road Runner BMX and Black Mountain records separate until a direct bridge is recovered.

AZ-PUB-0171

The March 1984 Bikes & Dirt Arizona track directory lists “Phoenix, Roadrunner Raceway” with published telephone numbers 602-867-2918 and 602-581-1436; the May 1985 BMX Action Arizona track directory again lists “Phoenix, Roadrunner Raceway,” this time with Michael J. Guidice and published telephone number 602-678-0960.

FACT — REPEATED PERIOD BMX DIRECTORY IDENTITY · B1 · HIGH FOR MARCH 1984 AND MAY 1985 PUBLISHED PHOENIX IDENTITY; SITE OPEN
Evidence boundary: This supports the reported August 2018 reopening of the locally named Colorado River BMX track off Highland Road under Turnell Henry and a preceding multi-year closure. It does not establish the exact beginning of that closure, prove uninterrupted identity or legal continuity from the May 1999 nonprofit record, connect the track to the 1984–1985 Bullhead City Kiwanis BMX or Bullhead BMX identities, or establish that the 2018 course footprint was geometrically identical to the present track.
