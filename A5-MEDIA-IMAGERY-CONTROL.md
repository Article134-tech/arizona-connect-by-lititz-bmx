## Governing rule

A visible image online is not automatically republishable.

For USA BMX galleries and event pages, the Atlas links to the original source and does not mirror gallery images.

For period publications, the Atlas links to the issue/source route and does not mirror scans.

For Lititz artifact photography, the artifact catalog and image metadata are governed internally, but the published dataset's own rights notice says public access does not grant blanket permission to reproduce the image. Therefore this A5 release records filename, dimensions, checksum and public artifact route without embedding the photograph.
