## Governing distinction

A4 does **not** mean every record has coordinates.

A4 means every governed census record now has an explicit geospatial state:
exact track point, site-level approximate point, historical point, area/city only, or no defensible point.

## Interstate control

Interstate BMX remains the important exception among current records.

Its current official address is the Mohave County Fairgrounds. The public A4 point therefore uses a **site-level fairgrounds coordinate** and is explicitly approximate. The separate High Desert 66 historical record has its own track-specific coordinate. This keeps geography useful without using same-address evidence to silently resolve the High Desert 66 → Interstate BMX succession question.

## Historical no-point control

Road Runner BMX, Chandler Jaycees BMX and Scottsdale Jaycees Rodeo BMX Park remain unpinned until their period locations can be registered without borrowing modern coordinates.

The ten recovery records also remain unpinned.
