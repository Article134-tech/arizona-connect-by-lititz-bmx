- Current USA BMX locates Chandler BMX at 298 S McQueen Rd / NW corner Frye and McQueen and describes it as the first BMX track the ABA opened for sanctioned racing.

- BMX News history timeline states Merl Mennenga started Chandler Jaycees in 1975 and that the track remained in the same location.
- Cross-source threshold clears same physical site; exact historical course geometry remains open.
