## Governing decision

Deepen Reid/Randolph spatial registration and Chino/Prescott comparative site history without treating contextual park records as named BMX identities or using later planning evidence to back-project an unresolved earlier site.

## Evidence gained

- **Randolph/Reid Park engineering frame:** City of Tucson construction-plan record `L-63-006` preserves a scanned 1963 “Master Plan and Drainage Randolph Park” with two plan sheets. It predates the later band-stage/BMX evidence and is admitted only as pre-BMX park geometry.
- **Randolph Park bandshell chronology:** City construction-plan record `C-66-003` is titled “Band Enclosure @ Randolph Park.” The archive explicitly says it is not scanned and has no downloadable document, so only the cataloged title/date is used.
- **City park history:** Tucson Parks and Recreation says Gene C. Reid created a makeshift band stage in 1964 and the bandshell was redeveloped into a full performance center in 1974.
- **Reid Park post-period registration:** City grading plan `GR-85-163` is a scanned 1985 “Reid Park Band Shell Area” record. It registers the bandshell sector after the recovered 1977 BMX memory but does not label a BMX course.
- **Old Home Manor:** a January 2009 public corridor study identifies Town-owned Old Home Manor Park as an 880-acre recreation property whose amenities included a BMX track. This is a dated site witness, not a sanctioned track identity.
- **Prescott Valley planning context:** a Town-hosted historical newspaper-clipping compilation records a March 31, 1999 decision declining a cost-free BMX-track proposal for Mountain Valley Park and suggesting Antelope Park or a future Agua Fria Park instead. This is later planning history, not evidence for the 1983–1984 Mile-Hi footprint.

## Reid / Randolph interpretation

The engineering sequence now brackets the bandshell reference through time:

- 1963 Randolph Park master-plan geometry;
- 1964 City-published makeshift band-stage history;
- 1966 cataloged Band Enclosure plan;
- 1974 City-published full performance-center redevelopment;
- separate 1977 attributed Randolph Park BMX photo-caption evidence and bandshell-vicinity personal memory;

This is a stronger registration frame, not an exact BMX footprint. No recovered municipal plan labels the historical BMX dirt course. The existing Gene C. Reid Park property-level point is therefore not moved.
