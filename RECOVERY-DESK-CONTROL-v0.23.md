# Arizona Connect v0.23 — Recovery Desk Control

## Purpose

Rebuild the Recovery layer as a first-class Arizona BMX Atlas interface without changing the governed track, location, timeline, claim, source, relationship, media, event, or research datasets.

## Locked recovery rules

- Recovery-open track records remain **10**.
- Census records without a governed point remain **13** and remain unpinned.
- Optional open Timeline lineage questions remain **5**.
- A stronger source may establish a bounded occurrence without resolving exact location, operating span, identity, or lineage.
- No city center is substituted for a missing track coordinate.
- No same-city, same-site, naming similarity, chronology, or geographic proximity is treated as proof of succession.
- Outer Limits remains at a specialist-directory corroboration threshold.
- Recovery status is not a Hall of Fame status or a negative claim about historical importance.

## Presentation rule

The Recovery desk separates: **what is established → what remains open → geographic treatment → evidence route / correction pathway**. Open boundaries are presented as useful research results rather than hidden interface defects.

## Preservation boundary

This checkpoint changes only the Recovery presentation layer plus Recovery-specific CSS/JavaScript and documentation. The approved Arizona Connect home, Atlas home, Geographic Absolute system, track profiles, Timeline, Relationships, Research Register and all governed data remain unchanged.
