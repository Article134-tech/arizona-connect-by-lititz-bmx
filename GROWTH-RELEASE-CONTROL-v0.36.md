# Arizona Connect v0.36 — Site + Lineage Recovery 1 Control

## Governing decision

Continue from sealed v0.35 without reopening or moving any inherited coordinate. This release is a site-and-lineage recovery pass, not a broad census-expansion pass. New geography may be admitted only when a named identity, dated evidence and a defensible property/site reference converge without requiring a lineage assumption.

## Evidence gained

- **Reid / Randolph Park BMX:** a public 1977 photo caption identifies “Randolph Park BMX Track, Tucson AZ.” A National BMX Hall of Fame interview with ABA founder Merl Mennenga separately recalls another Tucson BMX track as a downhill track in a city park run by Louis Stockmeyer. A community-history thread places a remembered Randolph Park course just north and west of the old bandshell, while the City of Tucson currently locates the Georges DeMeester Outdoor Performance Center in Reid Park. These sources narrow a recovery area but do **not** establish one exact course footprint or prove that all Randolph/Reid references describe one unchanged course.
- **Mile-Hi BMX — 1983:** BMX Plus! states that Brent and Brian Patterson taught BMX skills at the Mile-Hi BMX Summer Camp in Prescott. This strengthens the period activity/instructor record but does not resolve the Prescott versus Prescott Valley wording or exact camp/course site.
- **Mile High BMX — Chino Valley, 2002–2003:** official USA BMX event records independently document State Qualifiers at Mile High BMX on August 11, 2002 and August 10, 2003, both at the published `1020 W PALAMINO RD, CHINO VALLEY, ARIZONA 86323` address. The 2003 USA BMX driving-directions route encodes `34.7737852,-112.4656999`; the Town of Chino Valley currently identifies Memory Park at Palomino Road and N Road 1 West. This supports a bounded **Memory Park property-level SITE_POINT_APPROXIMATE**, not an exact dirt-course center.

## New governed identity

`AZ-TRK-0129 — Mile High BMX (Chino Valley)` is admitted as a separate historical identity.

It is **not merged** with:

- `AZ-TRK-0119 — Mile-Hi BMX` (1983–1984 Prescott / Prescott Valley evidence);
- `AZ-TRK-0118 — Chino Valley BMX` (1984 directory identity);
- the later BMX-site evidence associated with Old Home Manor.

No formal succession, rename, operator continuity or same-course claim is admitted.
