# Arizona Connect v0.28 — Recovery Search Log

## Threshold crossed
### London Bridge BMX
Official Federal Register evidence dated March 10, 1998 identifies Frank Sabatano as President of the London Bridge BMX Association in a bicycle-racing/jumping safety context. This independently strengthens the historical identity, but does not establish an exact track site or later succession.

### March 2002 regional ABA directory
Cycling Utah's March 2002 directory lists Sara Park BMX (Lake Havasu City), Parker BMX (Parker), Coppertown BMX (San Manuel), and RAM BMX (Tucson). This creates a dated second snapshot for Parker, Coppertown and RAM and a dated Sara Park identity after the separate 1998 London Bridge record.

## Threshold not crossed
### Thunder Raceway
Searches recovered same-name motor-racing material in Show Low. It is not used as evidence for the historical BMX track. Identity separation and exact site remain open.

### Outer Limits BMX
The specialist-directory listing remains the strongest currently admitted evidence. No period or first-party corroboration crossed the public-admission threshold in this pass.

### Exact sites
No exact historical coordinates were recovered for London Bridge, Sara Park, Parker, Coppertown or RAM. They remain unpinned under Geographic Absolute.
