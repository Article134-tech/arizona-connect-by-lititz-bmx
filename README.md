## Governing evidence rule

**No factual display record is permitted without a governed evidence/source path.**

A source route does not make every interpretation true. Authority, confidence, evidence character, date precision, location precision, rights state, and the evidence boundary remain part of the record.

The Atlas remains an **ACTIVE HISTORICAL CENSUS — NOT COMPLETE**. Working record counts do not claim historical completeness.

## Geographic Absolute

Arizona geography is authoritative, not decorative.
- Arizona boundary and plotted points use one georeferenced frame.
- No surrounding-state or Mexico geography is used inside the Arizona location layer.
- Plotted markers come from governed registered latitude/longitude.
- Exact, site-approximate, and historical points remain distinguishable.
- Records without defensible coordinates stay unpinned.
- No city-center or visually convenient coordinate substitution is allowed unless a governed record explicitly authorizes an approximate point.

## No-silent-merge rule

Similar names, shared cities, plausible chronology, or geographic proximity do not automatically establish predecessor/successor identity, rename continuity, ownership, or operation continuity. Open lineage questions remain visibly open until evidence closes them.

## Recovery

Recovery is a governed state, not an embarrassment or filler queue. Stronger evidence can establish part of a record while exact site, operating span, identity, or lineage remains OPEN.

## Rights / media

The A5 register contains 19 governed media routes. Third-party imagery is linked rather than copied unless display rights are explicitly controlled. Visible online does not equal cleared to republish.
