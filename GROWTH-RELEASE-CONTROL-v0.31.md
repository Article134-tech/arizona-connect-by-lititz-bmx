# Arizona Connect v0.31 - Census Expansion 1 Control

## Governing decision

v0.31 is the first growth release to expand the governed Arizona track census itself rather than only deepening the existing 25 records.

The expansion is evidence-led. A period directory occurrence may establish a historical track identity and city association without earning a geographic point. Geography remains a separate governed layer.

- Public claims: 95 -> 109
- Governed track records: 25 -> 35
- Distinct governed source IDs behind public claims: 69 -> 74
- Published Timeline milestones: 57 -> 62
- Optional open Timeline questions: 5 unchanged
- Governed events: 24 -> 26
- Mapped records: 14 -> 15
- Intentionally unpinned records: 11 -> 20
- Relationship edges: 15 unchanged

## Navajo County Fairgrounds / Holbrook recovery

AZ-TRK-0124 is added as a site-level historical record for the Navajo County Fairgrounds BMX facility in Holbrook.

Period Tribune-News evidence supports:

- a newly completed BMX facility and opening-era racing at the fairgrounds in 1989;
- the BMXpress name at the county fairgrounds in 1991;
- the Painted Desert BMX Track name at the same county-fairgrounds property in 1992.

The record preserves that documented sequence without claiming a recovered formal BMXpress -> Painted Desert rename date or uninterrupted sanction continuity.

The geographic marker is SITE_POINT_APPROXIMATE at the fairgrounds property, not an exact historical track-center.

## SARA Park -> Lake Havasu City BMX

Official Lake Havasu City minutes document a September 20, 2018 BMX-track renaming ceremony and state that the new name would be Lake Havasu City BMX.

This closes the SARA Park BMX Raceway -> Lake Havasu City BMX naming transition at the already-governed shared physical site. It does not close the earlier London Bridge BMX -> SARA Park predecessor question.

## Manzanita 2008 event reconciliation

AZ-PUB-0097 adds an independent official result route for the already-governed April 2, 2008 Manzanita BMX Raceway event.

No duplicate event or duplicate Timeline milestone is created. The new claim is crosswalked into existing AZ-EVT-0004 and AZ-TL-0014.

## Geographic Absolute

- The 14 v0.30 published coordinate records are preserved exactly.
- The Arizona-only boundary SVG and geographic mask are unchanged.
- Holbrook is the only new plotted record.
- All 15 plotted points must pass the Arizona mask containment test.
- Nine new 1984 directory identities remain deliberately unpinned.
- A city association is not a coordinate.
- A fairgrounds-property coordinate is not an exact track-center.

## Preservation boundary

v0.31 does not:

- redesign the approved Arizona Connect launcher, Atlas home, profile family, Timeline, Relationships, Recovery or Methodology language;
- merge historical identities because they share a city or later site;
- create Hall of Fame status;
- copy protected third-party imagery;
- turn directory evidence into exact geography;
- close London Bridge -> SARA Park, RAM -> Manzanita, or other unresolved lineage paths without direct evidence.
