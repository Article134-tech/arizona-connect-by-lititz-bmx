Finding a track name is not the same as finding the track. A directory can prove that a name was in use. A city record can prove that an operator leased land. A dated sanctioning-body occurrence can prove that racing happened. None of those automatically proves the exact footprint, complete operating span, or organizational lineage.

THE RECOVERY GATE

Evidence found does not mean recovery closed.

01What is established?

Name, city, dated occurrence, directory listing, lease record, or another bounded fact.

02What is still open?

Exact site, dates, identity, lineage, footprint, succession, or source corroboration.

03What would change status?

New inspectable evidence that crosses the stated threshold. Not resemblance. Not geography. Not assumption.

PROMOTION RULES

How an open record earns its way forward.

01Source before certainty

A recovery lead can become source-governed without becoming geographically or chronologically complete.

02Coordinate is its own claim

A city association never becomes an exact track point unless location evidence supports that point.

03Lineage needs lineage evidence

Same city, same property, similar name, or chronological proximity does not prove organizational succession.

04Correction stays open

Every unresolved record can improve when stronger evidence arrives. The boundary tells us exactly what to test.
