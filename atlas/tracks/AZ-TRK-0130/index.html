WHAT WE KNOW

What we know

The May 1985 BMX Action Arizona track directory lists “Phoenix, Desert Edge BMX” with Scott Koby and published telephone number 602-861-6070. The same issue separately lists Heritage Park BMX under Phoenix with Kathy Scott and also includes 602-861-6070 among its published telephone numbers. That shared number is preserved as a relationship signal only; it does not establish that the tracks were the same facility, shared an operator, represented a rename or succession, or occupied the same site. No exact Desert Edge site or adjacent-issue repetition has been recovered.

Historical Tracks / Period Directory Identity

The May 1985 BMX Action Arizona track directory lists “Phoenix, Desert Edge BMX” with Scott Koby and published telephone number 602-861-6070.

Limits: This establishes Desert Edge BMX as a separately named Phoenix historical track identity in the May 1985 period directory and preserves Scott Koby as the published contact. The same issue separately prints Heritage Park BMX with Kathy Scott and also includes 602-861-6070 among its numbers; that shared number is a relationship signal only. It does not prove the two names refer to the same facility, a rename, succession, shared ownership or operation, the same physical site, or uninterrupted activity. No exact Desert Edge address or course point is published.
