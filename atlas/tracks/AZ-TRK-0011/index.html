AZ-PUB-0061 · A2 · HIGH

Tracks / Municipal History

Show Low City Council minutes state that the Show Low BMX track was originally constructed in 2010, hosted weekly races and sanctioned events for several years, and stopped holding official races after 2017 when revised sanctioning standards left the track out of compliance.

Limits: The 2021 municipal retrospective supports the 2010 construction date, prior sanctioned activity and post-2017 official-race gap. It does not establish uninterrupted operation in every intervening year or prove continuity with the historical Thunder Raceway name.

AZ-PUB-0062 · A2 · HIGH

Operators / Municipal Partnership

On January 5, 2021, the Show Low City Council unanimously approved an agreement with Show Low BMX, LLC for reconstruction and operation of the BMX track at Nikolaus Homestead Park.

Limits: The minutes establish the council action, the operator entity and the Nikolaus Homestead Park project. They do not by themselves establish the exact completion date of reconstruction or lineage to Thunder Raceway.
