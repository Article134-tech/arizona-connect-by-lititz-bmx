AZ-PUB-0058 · A2 + A1 · HIGH

Historical Tracks / Municipal Record

On June 16, 2003, the City of Tucson approved a lease with Desert Sunset BMX, Inc. for operation of a dirt bicycle track at the Harrison Landfill; USA BMX later listed Desert Sunset BMX in Tucson for Olympic Day on June 23, 2013.

Limits: These sources establish a 2003 Tucson lease for Desert Sunset BMX, Inc. at Harrison Landfill and a separate 2013 USA BMX track/event listing. They do not prove uninterrupted operation throughout the intervening decade or exact later lineage.

AZ-PUB-0186 · A2 + C1 · HIGH FOR DISTINCT-FACILITY IDENTITY

Historical Tracks / Distinct Facility Evidence

A 2009 regional transportation study separately mapped Manzanita Park BMX and Desert Sunset BMX, while a contemporaneous Tucson cycling inventory separately listed Manzanita BMX Raceway and Desert Sunset BMX Track with different recurring race nights. Together, the records support that Manzanita and Desert Sunset were distinct Tucson-area BMX facilities rather than alternate names for one track.

Limits: The sources support distinct facility identity in the 2009–2011 evidence period. They do not establish exact historical course footprints, ownership, full operating spans, sanction continuity, RAM BMX lineage, succession to later Tucson facilities, or any new Geographic Absolute coordinate.
