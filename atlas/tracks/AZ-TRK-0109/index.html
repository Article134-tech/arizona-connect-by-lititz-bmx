AZ-PUB-0055 · B1 · HIGH FOR BMX DIRECTORY LISTING

Historical Tracks / Period Directory

A BMX track guide last modified in 1998 listed Thunder Raceway in Show Low, Arizona as an ABA BMX track and named Chris Tippie as the contact.

Limits: This proves a 1998 BMX-directory listing named Thunder Raceway in Show Low. It must not be silently conflated with the present-day Deuce of Clubs Thunder Raceway motor-racing facility without location/identity evidence.

AZ-PUB-0090 · B1 + B2 · HIGH FOR NAME-CONFLICT CONTROL

Historical Tracks / Same-name Conflict

A 2013 IMCA points document separately lists “Thunder Raceway — Show Low, AZ” in an auto-racing competition context, while the 1998 BMX directory independently lists Thunder Raceway in Show Low as an ABA BMX track.

Limits: The two sources prove that the Thunder Raceway name was used in Show Low in BMX and later motor-racing contexts. They do not prove whether the facilities were geographically identical, related, renamed, or completely separate. No modern motor-racing coordinate may be assigned to the historical BMX record from this evidence.
