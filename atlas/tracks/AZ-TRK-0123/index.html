HISTORICAL TRACK PROFILE · Peoria, ARIZONA

Peoria Dunes BMX

A 1984 period directory preserves Peoria Dunes BMX with Gene Garcez. Attributed rider memories place Peoria Dunes activity in 1982 and through the 1980s. May and June 1985 directories list the separate Peoria identity Hills ’n Spills BMX with Gene Garcez and the same published telephone number. That continuity signal is not treated as proof of a formal rename or identical physical course.

Historical Tracks / Period Directory

The March 1984 issue of Bikes & Dirt lists “Peoria, Peoria Dunes BMX” in its Arizona BMX track directory.

Limits: This establishes the Peoria Dunes BMX identity and Peoria, Arizona association in a March 1984 period directory. It does not establish an exact track footprint, full operating span, ownership, or continuity with any later track identity.

AZ-PUB-0126 · C2 · HIGH FOR ATTRIBUTED STATEMENT; MEDIUM FOR OPENING-ERA WITNESS

Historical Tracks / Attributed Rider Memory

In a 2015 memorial comment, Ken Barrier recalled meeting Ted Jorgensen in 1982 and racing the first night at Peoria Dunes before being invited to join Jorgensen’s team.

Limits: This preserves Barrier’s attributed personal recollection and supports an opening-era Peoria Dunes witness in 1982. It does not independently establish the exact opening date, sanction, track site, ownership, or that “first night” was the facility’s formal first-ever race.

AZ-PUB-0133 · B1 · HIGH FOR PUBLISHED JUNE IDENTITY + DIRECTORY-LEVEL CONTINUITY SIGNAL

Historical Tracks / Period Directory Transition

The June 1985 BMX Plus! track directory lists Hills ’n Spills BMX in Peoria with Gene Garcez; the printed telephone number matches the 1984 Peoria Dunes BMX directory listing.

Limits: The matching city, published contact and telephone number create a strong directory-level continuity signal between Peoria Dunes BMX and Hills ’n Spills BMX. They do not prove a formal rename, identical physical course, uninterrupted operation, ownership or organizational succession, so Hills ’n Spills BMX is preserved as a separate track identity.

AZ-PUB-0135 · B1 · HIGH FOR REPEATED MAY–JUNE 1985 DIRECTORY IDENTITY / DIRECT PAGE VERIFIED

Historical Tracks / Period Directory Continuity

The May 1985 BMX Action directory lists Hills ’n Spills BMX in Peoria with Gene Garcez; the June 1985 BMX Plus! directory repeats the same identity, contact and published telephone number.

Limits: This moves the earliest directly recovered Hills ’n Spills BMX directory occurrence from June back to May 1985 and proves consecutive recovered directory continuity for that printed identity. It does not prove a formal rename from Peoria Dunes BMX, an identical physical course, uninterrupted operation, ownership or organizational succession.
