HISTORICAL TRACK PROFILE · Tucson, ARIZONA

Manzanita BMX Raceway

A period 1984 directory lists Manzanita Park BMX in Tucson; 2003 and 2008 records preserve Manzanita BMX Raceway; Pima County documented a BMX track inside Winston Reynolds–Manzanita Park in 2007; and a dated 2011 event notice names Manzanita BMX Raceway at 5200 S Westover Ave. A park-level approximate point is registered, not an exact historical track-center. RAM BMX continuity remains open.

AZ-PUB-0093 · A2 · HIGH FOR PARK-LEVEL BMX SITE

Historical Tracks / Municipal Site Record

Pima County’s May 9, 2007 Southwest Infrastructure Plan describes Winston Reynolds–Manzanita Pool District Park as including a BMX track and says future park plans included a restroom and a ramada at the BMX track.

Boundary: This establishes a BMX track inside Winston Reynolds–Manzanita Park by May 2007. It does not identify the exact track-center coordinate, prove uninterrupted operation, or by itself prove that every RAM BMX / Manzanita BMX name refers to the same organization.

AZ-PUB-0186 · A2 + C1 · HIGH FOR DISTINCT-FACILITY IDENTITY

Historical Tracks / Distinct Facility Evidence

A 2009 regional transportation study separately mapped Manzanita Park BMX and Desert Sunset BMX, while a contemporaneous Tucson cycling inventory separately listed Manzanita BMX Raceway and Desert Sunset BMX Track with different recurring race nights. Together, the records support that Manzanita and Desert Sunset were distinct Tucson-area BMX facilities rather than alternate names for one track.

Boundary: The sources support distinct facility identity in the 2009–2011 evidence period. They do not establish exact historical course footprints, ownership, full operating spans, sanction continuity, RAM BMX lineage, succession to later Tucson facilities, or any new Geographic Absolute coordinate.
