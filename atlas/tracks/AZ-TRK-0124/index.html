WHAT WE KNOW

What we know

Period Tribune-News reporting documents a new BMX facility at the Navajo County Fairgrounds in 1989, the BMXpress name at the fairgrounds in 1991, and the Painted Desert BMX Track name at the same fairgrounds in 1992. This site-level record preserves the documented name sequence without claiming an unrecovered formal rename date or uninterrupted sanction continuity.

AZ-PUB-0098 · B1 · HIGH FOR NEW 1989 FAIRGROUNDS BMX FACILITY

Historical Tracks / Period Newspaper

A 1989 Tribune-News report preserved in the Arizona Memory Project describes a newly completed BMX facility at the Navajo County Fairgrounds in Holbrook and reports the first races at the new facility.

Limits: This establishes a newly completed BMX track at the Navajo County Fairgrounds in Holbrook in 1989. The archival newspaper evidence supports the property-level site and opening-era racing, but not an exact track-center coordinate within the fairgrounds or a formal later rename date.

AZ-PUB-0099 · B1 · HIGH FOR 1991 BMXPRESS NAME + FAIRGROUNDS SITE

Historical Tracks / Period Newspaper

The Tribune-News of June 26, 1991 reported racing at the “BMXpress track at the county fairgrounds in Holbrook,” including a June 22 race day and continuing meets through October.

Limits: This establishes the BMXpress name in active use at the Navajo County Fairgrounds in June 1991. It does not by itself prove the formal relationship between the BMXpress name and the later Painted Desert BMX Track name.

AZ-PUB-0204 · B1 · HIGH FOR NOVEMBER 1991 PUBLISHED BMXPRESS IDENTITY + CONTACT

Historical Tracks / Period Directory

The November 1991 GO: The Rider’s Manual directory lists “Navajo County BMXPRESS” in Holbrook with Paul DoBell and telephone number 602-524-6407; a separate “NEW TRACKS” box in the same issue lists Holbrook, Arizona / BMX Press / Paul Dobell with the same number.

Limits: This independently preserves the BMXPRESS identity, Holbrook locality, Paul DoBell/Dobell contact and telephone number in a national BMX publication. The “NEW TRACKS” directory label is not treated as proof that the physical Holbrook facility first opened in 1991; period local newspaper evidence documents the fairgrounds BMX facility in 1989.

AZ-PUB-0205 · B1 · HIGH FOR REPORTED CONSTRUCTION SCALE + NBL ENGINEERING ROLE

Historical Tracks / Community Construction

A 1989 Tribune-News report says another 1,500 to 1,600 yards of fill dirt were needed to complete the Holbrook BMX track’s berms, curves and jumps, that more than 110 truck trips carried fill, and that National Bike League representative Bob Tedesco was in Holbrook to engineer the construction.

Limits: This preserves the period newspaper’s reported construction quantities, truck activity and Bob Tedesco/NBL engineering role. It does not reconstruct the final course geometry, establish a licensed engineer-of-record relationship beyond the wording of the report, or prove which individual features survived later changes.
