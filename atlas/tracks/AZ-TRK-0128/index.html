HISTORICAL TRACK PROFILE · Phoenix, ARIZONA

Heritage Park BMX

The May 1985 BMX Action Arizona directory lists “Phoenix, Heritage Park BMX” with Kathy Scott and published telephone numbers 602-861-6070 and 602-578-5620. The recovered June 1985 BMX Plus! Arizona directory does not repeat Heritage Park BMX and instead lists Mesa BMX with Kathy Zosky and Debbie Cotton. v0.44 withdraws the earlier Prescott municipal-park association and point. Heritage Park BMX remains a historical Phoenix identity with exact site, operator role, opening/closing span, sanction continuity and relationships to other Phoenix tracks unresolved.

Historical Tracks / Period Directory Identity

The May 1985 BMX Action Arizona track directory lists “Phoenix, Heritage Park BMX” with Kathy Scott and published telephone numbers 602-861-6070 and 602-578-5620. The recovered June 1985 BMX Plus! Arizona directory does not repeat Heritage Park BMX; it instead lists Mesa BMX with Kathy Zosky and Debbie Cotton.

Limits: This corrects the v0.35 Prescott / Kathy Zosky / Debbie Cotton attribution. The recovered evidence establishes a May 1985 Heritage Park BMX directory identity under Phoenix only. It does not establish the exact physical site, ownership or operator role, opening or closing dates, sanction continuity, or a relationship to Desert Edge BMX, Roadrunner Raceway, or another Phoenix-area track.

AZ-PUB-0137 · SUPPORTING EVIDENCE · A2

Supporting Evidence · Historical Tracks / Municipal Site Context

Arizona State Parks’ Land and Water Conservation Fund register does document City of Prescott “Heritage Park” projects in 1979 and 1984, but those Prescott municipal-park records are not site evidence for Heritage Park BMX after correction of the period BMX directory identity to Phoenix.

Limits: The Prescott Heritage Park records remain valid municipal-history evidence. No recovered source bridges that Prescott park to the May 1985 Phoenix Heritage Park BMX identity, so the records must not be used to infer the BMX track location.

Historical Tracks / Geographic Registration

The previously published Prescott Heritage Park location at 34.6133572, -112.4404465 is withdrawn from the Arizona BMX map. Heritage Park BMX is now documented to Phoenix only because the corrected period evidence places the track identity under Phoenix and no defensible Phoenix site coordinate has been recovered.
