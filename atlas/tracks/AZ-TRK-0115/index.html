Tri Valley BMX

Exact site, role history and later lineage remain open.

AZ-PUB-0110 · B1 · HIGH FOR SEPTEMBER 1982 DIRECTORY OCCURRENCE

Historical Tracks / Period Directory

The September 1982 premiere issue of Bikes & Dirt lists Tri Valley in Casa Grande and names Jeff Pritchett as the directory contact.

Boundary: This establishes a Tri Valley BMX directory occurrence in Casa Grande by September 1982, earlier than the March 1984 snapshot. A directory contact is not automatically an owner or operator, and the two snapshots do not by themselves prove uninterrupted operation, an exact track footprint, or later continuity.
