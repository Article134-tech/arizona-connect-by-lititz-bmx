HISTORICAL TRACK PROFILE · Casa Grande, ARIZONA

Tri Valley BMX

Period directories preserve Tri Valley BMX in Casa Grande in September 1982, March 1984 and April 1984. The September issue prints Jeff Pritchett while the direct-verified April page prints Jeff Prindle; the archive preserves that variation rather than silently normalizing it. Exact site, role history and later lineage remain open.

AZ-PUB-0101 · B1 · HIGH FOR MARCH 1984 DIRECTORY OCCURRENCE

Historical Tracks / Period Directory

The March 1984 issue of Bikes & Dirt lists “Casa Grande, Tri Valley BMX” in its Arizona BMX track directory.

Limits: This establishes the Tri Valley BMX identity and Casa Grande, Arizona association in a March 1984 period directory. It does not establish an exact track footprint, full operating span, ownership, or continuity with any later track identity.

AZ-PUB-0110 · B1 · HIGH FOR SEPTEMBER 1982 DIRECTORY OCCURRENCE

Historical Tracks / Period Directory

The September 1982 premiere issue of Bikes & Dirt lists Tri Valley in Casa Grande and names Jeff Pritchett as the directory contact.

Limits: This establishes a Tri Valley BMX directory occurrence in Casa Grande by September 1982, earlier than the March 1984 snapshot. A directory contact is not automatically an owner or operator, and the two snapshots do not by themselves prove uninterrupted operation, an exact track footprint, or later continuity.

Historical Tracks / Period Directory Contact

The April 1984 Bikes & Dirt directory again lists Tri Valley in Casa Grande and prints Jeff Prindle as the published contact.
