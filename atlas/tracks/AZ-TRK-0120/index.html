HISTORICAL TRACK PROFILE · Bullhead City, ARIZONA

Bullhead City Kiwanis BMX

Bullhead City Kiwanis BMX is preserved in 1984 and May 1985 directory snapshots; direct page inspection corrects the March contact to Kirk Jongsma. A June 1985 directory then lists the separate identity Bullhead BMX with John Schneider at the same published telephone number used in May. This is not converted into a formal rename, same-site or organizational-succession claim.
