Tracks / Lineage

USA BMX's 2024 Hall of Fame profile for Debbie Kelley says Debbie and Pete Kelley took over Road Runner BMX in the mid-1980s and renamed it Black Mountain.

Limits: Exact rename year, legal entity continuity, and site continuity remain separate questions.

AZ-PUB-0015 · A1 · HIGH

Events / State Ecosystem

USA BMX's 2019 Winter Nationals report records Arizona riders from multiple cities and competition levels at Black Mountain, including Phoenix, Tucson, Chandler, Kingman, Gilbert, Mesa, Avondale, Surprise, Tempe and others.

Limits: This supports a broad statewide participation pattern at that event; do not turn every listed rider into a heritage or Hall of Fame candidate.
