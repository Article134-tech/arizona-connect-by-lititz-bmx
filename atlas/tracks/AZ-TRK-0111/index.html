HISTORICAL TRACK PROFILE · Lake Havasu City, ARIZONA

Sara Park BMX Raceway

SARA Park BMX Raceway is historically evidenced in Lake Havasu City, and v0.30 now registers the documented physical BMX track site. The physical-site point does not close the London Bridge → SARA Park → current Lake Havasu City BMX identity/organization question.

WHAT WE KNOW

What we know

Dated 2002 and 2013 occurrences preserve the SARA Park BMX identity; a 2017 retrospective places the raceway at 7260 Sara Park Lane and reports a circa-2015 rebuild that retained asphalt turns. The historical physical site is now registered at the same governed point as the current Lake Havasu City BMX track.

AZ-PUB-0057 · A1 · HIGH

Historical Tracks / Official Event

USA BMX's June 20, 2013 Olympic Day announcement listed Sara Park BMX Raceway in Lake Havasu City, Arizona for an Olympic Day race on June 27, 2013.

Limits: This proves the Sara Park BMX Raceway name, Lake Havasu City association and dated 2013 USA BMX event listing. It does not by itself establish the exact rename/continuity path to current Lake Havasu City BMX.

AZ-PUB-0086 · C1-local-retrospective · MEDIUM-HIGH

Historical Tracks / Local Retrospective

A September 6, 2017 RiverScene feature states that SARA Park BMX Raceway was built in the early 1990s, was rebuilt from the ground up about two years before the article while retaining its asphalt turns, and was located at 7260 Sara Park Lane in Lake Havasu City.

Limits: This is a dated local retrospective, not a period construction record. It supports the reported early-1990s era, 2015-era rebuild and 2017 address, but it does not establish an exact opening year, original builder, London Bridge BMX identity, or uninterrupted organizational continuity.

Historical Tracks / Operators

Historical Tracks / Municipal Transition

Lake Havasu City Parks and Recreation Advisory Board minutes record that a BMX-track renaming ceremony was held on September 20, 2018 and state that the new name would be “Lake Havasu City BMX.”
