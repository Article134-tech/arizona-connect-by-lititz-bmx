HISTORICAL TRACK PROFILE · Prescott Valley, ARIZONA

Mile-Hi BMX

BMX Plus! says Brent and Brian Patterson taught BMX skills at the Mile-Hi BMX Summer Camp in Prescott in 1983. March and April 1984 directories list Mile-Hi BMX under Prescott Valley with Scott Sandberg. An August 1980 Prescott Valley fact sheet separately lists Sandberg’s Family Restaurant and Scott’s Bike Shop; a reproduced 1997 obituary identifies a Scott Sandberg of Prescott Valley as a brother in the Sandberg family associated with the restaurant. Town-hosted historical newspaper clippings separately record a Ray Sandberg in Prescott Valley municipal leadership through March 23, 1984. These records strengthen the recovery convergence only: the civic Ray is not equated to the obituary father, the obituary Scott is not equated to the BMX contact, and no business role, phone subscriber, or track site is inferred. The exact camp/track footprint and any relationship to later Chino Valley “Mile High BMX” remain unresolved.

AZ-PUB-0165 · A2 · HIGH FOR BUSINESS-NAME / PRESCOTT VALLEY LISTING

Historical Tracks / Municipal Record

An August 1, 1980 Prescott Valley fact sheet preserved in the Arizona Memory Project lists both “Sandberg’s Family Restaurant” and “Scott’s Bike Shop” among businesses within Prescott Valley.

Boundary: This establishes that both business names were present in Prescott Valley by August 1980, providing locality context four years before the Mile-Hi directory lists Scott Sandberg. It does not establish ownership of Scott’s Bike Shop, identify Scott Sandberg as owner/operator of either business, connect either business to Mile-Hi BMX, reverse the published 602-772-8050 number, or locate the track or Summer Camp footprint.

Historical Tracks / Family Context

A reproduced 1997 obituary for Diane Sandberg-Laipple says she moved with her parents Ray and Ruby Sandberg to Prescott Valley in 1976 to open a family restaurant, continued helping at Sandberg’s Family Restaurant, and identifies Scott Sandberg of Prescott Valley as her brother.

Boundary: This independently documents a Scott Sandberg of Prescott Valley as a member of the Sandberg family associated with Sandberg’s Family Restaurant. AZ-PUB-0167 · B1 / A2 PRESERVATION · HIGH FOR REPORTED CIVIC ROLES / DATES; CONTEXT ONLY FOR MILE-HI

Historical Tracks / Municipal Civic Context

A Prescott Valley historical newspaper-clipping compilation records Ray Sandberg as vice mayor in 1981, uses “Mayor Ray Sandberg” in 1982–1983 entries, and records Councilman Ray Sandberg’s formal resignation from the Town Council on March 23, 1984.

Boundary: This independently establishes a Ray Sandberg in Prescott Valley municipal leadership during the period immediately preceding and overlapping the 1984 Mile-Hi directory contacts. It does not establish that this Ray Sandberg is the same Ray Sandberg named as Scott Sandberg’s father in the 1997 obituary, identify the 1984 Mile-Hi contact as the obituary’s Scott Sandberg, establish any ownership or employment at Sandberg’s Family Restaurant or Scott’s Bike Shop, connect municipal office to Mile-Hi BMX, reverse 602-772-8050, or locate the track or Summer Camp footprint.
