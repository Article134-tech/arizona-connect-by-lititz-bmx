AZ-PUB-0191 · A1 · HIGH FOR 2020 HIGH DESERT 66 NAME + FAIRGROUNDS ADDRESS WITNESS

Historical Tracks / Dated Event

A September 27, 2020 USA BMX event record at 2600 Fairgrounds Blvd in Kingman contains event-description text naming High Desert 66 and directing riders to the High Desert 66 BMX Facebook page.

Limits: The dated event content establishes High Desert 66 wording at the 2600 Fairgrounds Blvd venue on September 27, 2020. The current USA BMX page shell now displays Interstate BMX; that present-day shell label is not backdated as a 2020 name. AZ-PUB-0192 · A2 · HIGH FOR REPORTED ATTENDANCE RANGE AND TOURISM SUPPORT

Events / Local Impact

Kingman's 2021 Economic Development and Tourism annual report says the High Desert Route 66 Classic Nationals BMX Tournament at the Mohave County Fairgrounds drew roughly 500 competitors and 2,200–3,000 spectators, and that the tourism office assisted with hotel room blocks.

Limits: This establishes the City-reported 2021 event scale and tourism-office support. The attendance figures are reported estimates, not independently audited ticket counts, and the record does not establish a recurring annual attendance level.
