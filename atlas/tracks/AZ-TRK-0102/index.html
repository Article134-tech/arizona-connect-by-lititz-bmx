AZ-PUB-0172 · B2 + B1 + A1 · MEDIUM-HIGH FOR SAME SITE

Historical Tracks / Site History

A cross-source record supports same-site continuity between Chandler Jaycees BMX and today’s Chandler BMX: a BMX history timeline says Merl Mennenga started Chandler Jaycees in 1975 and that the track remained in the same location; a June 1987 BMX Plus! retrospective identifies Chandler Jaycees as the ABA’s first track; and current USA BMX places Chandler BMX at Frye and McQueen while describing it as the first BMX track the ABA opened for sanctioned racing.

Limits: This supports Chandler Jaycees BMX and current Chandler BMX occupying the same physical track site at the Frye/McQueen ground. It does not establish the exact 1975 or 1979–1983 dirt-course center, identical course geometry through time, every operator or sanction transition, uninterrupted organizational identity under one legal entity, or an exact 1975 street-address formulation. The historical map marker represents the shared site approximately, while the current Chandler track location is documented exactly.

AZ-PUB-0203 · B1 · HIGH FOR REPEATED MARCH–APRIL 1984 IDENTITY + CONTACT

Historical Tracks / Period Directory Continuity

The March and April 1984 Bikes & Dirt Arizona directories both list Chandler Jaycees BMX with Dennis Kalsow; March publishes 602-831-2511 and 602-963-0492, while April repeats 602-831-2511.

Limits: This establishes repeated adjacent-issue Chandler Jaycees BMX identity and Dennis Kalsow contact wording and preserves the published telephone-number detail. It does not by itself establish Kalsow’s ownership or operator role, exact historical course geometry, or every organizational transition to the current Chandler BMX identity.
