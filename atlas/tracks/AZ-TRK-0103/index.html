AZ-PUB-0185 · B1 · HIGH FOR PERIOD REPORT

Historical Tracks / Period Publication

A June 1987 BMX Plus! report states that the Scottsdale jail was directly across the street from the Scottsdale Jaycees Rodeo BMX Park during the Winter Nationals weekend.

Limits: The account establishes an across-the-street spatial relationship between the BMX venue and the Scottsdale jail. It does not identify the jail's exact 1986 street address, the separating roadway, BMX parcel boundaries, course center, or justify a Location confidence coordinate.

AZ-PUB-0207 · B1 + A2/C1 LOCAL ARCHIVE · MEDIUM-HIGH FOR RODEO/STADIUM COMPLEX; EXACT BMX COURSE POINT OPEN

Historical Tracks / Geographic Registration

BMX Plus! identifies Scottsdale Jaycees Rodeo BMX Park as the 1986 Winter Nationals venue, while a Scottsdale Historical Society digital record places the Jaycees Rodeo Arena on the east side of 78th Street north of Osborn Road and says it was later removed for Scottsdale Stadium expansion. Together, those records narrow the BMX venue to the Jaycees rodeo / Scottsdale Stadium complex.

Limits: This narrows the historical BMX venue to the documented Jaycees rodeo / Scottsdale Stadium complex. It does not establish the exact BMX course center, parcel boundary, separating roadway from the period jail, or a Location confidence coordinate.
