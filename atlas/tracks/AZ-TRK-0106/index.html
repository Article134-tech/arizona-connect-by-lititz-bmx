AZ-PUB-0052 · B1 · HIGH FOR DIRECTORY LISTING

Historical Tracks / Period Directory

The same 1998 BMX track guide listed London Bridge BMX in Lake Havasu, Arizona in its American Bicycle Association section, with Frank Sabatano as the named contact.

Boundary: This proves the period directory listing and city association. It does not prove that London Bridge BMX was the same track or organization as later Sara Park BMX Raceway or current Lake Havasu City BMX.

AZ-PUB-0081 · A2 · HIGH

Historical Tracks / Federal Record

On March 10, 1998, the Federal Register identified Frank Sabatano as President of the London Bridge BMX Association and recorded his recommendation that bicycle helmets account for more serious accidents that could result from child bicycle racing or jumping.

Boundary: This independently corroborates the London Bridge BMX Association identity and Sabatano's leadership in a BMX-racing context. It does not establish the track's exact Lake Havasu City site, opening or closing dates, or continuity to Sara Park BMX Raceway or current Lake Havasu City BMX.
