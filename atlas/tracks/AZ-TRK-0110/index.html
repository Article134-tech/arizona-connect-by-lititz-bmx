AZ-PUB-0056 · B1 + C1 · HIGH FOR PERIOD/REGIONAL LISTING

Historical Tracks / Period Directory

A BMX track guide last modified in 1998 listed RAM BMX in Tucson, Arizona in its ABA section; a separate regional BMX directory also listed RAM BMX in Tucson.

Boundary: The directories support the RAM BMX name and Tucson association. Exact site, operating dates and relationship to later Tucson tracks remain open.

AZ-PUB-0085 · B1 + C1 · HIGH FOR REPEATED PERIOD LISTING

Historical Tracks / Dated Directory

RAM BMX is independently listed in Tucson, Arizona in both the September 1998 BMX track guide and the March 2002 Cycling Utah ABA track directory.

Boundary: Repeated listings support the RAM BMX name and Tucson association across the two dated snapshots. They do not prove uninterrupted operation, exact site, or a relationship to Sports Park BMX, Tucson BMX, Desert Sunset BMX, or Manzanita BMX Raceway.

AZ-PUB-0088 · D1-attributed-witness · MEDIUM

Historical Tracks / Personal Truth

In a July 2010 condolence posted to Christopher Moon’s obituary, Bob Duncklee and Irma Duncklee-Woodward recalled their children racing BMX with Chris at “RAM BMX in Manzanita Park.”

Boundary: Publish only as an attributed retrospective memory. It materially supports Manzanita Park as a RAM BMX site context, but it does not establish the exact track footprint, operating dates, sanction status, or continuity to Manzanita BMX Raceway / Roland Thomas Widman BMX Park.

AZ-PUB-0089 · A2 · HIGH FOR CURRENT PARK / BMX FACILITY CONTEXT

Tracks / Municipal Site Context

Pima County currently identifies Winston Reynolds–Manzanita Park at 5200 S Westover Ave in Tucson and lists the Roland Thomas Widman BMX Park among its facilities.

Boundary: This establishes the current official Manzanita Park address and a current BMX facility in the park. It does not prove that historical RAM BMX occupied the same exact footprint or that RAM BMX and the present BMX facility are one continuous organization.
