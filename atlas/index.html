EVIDENCE STATUS

Uncertainty stays visible.

The Atlas does not use one visual treatment for evidence of different strength. A current directory record, a period historical identity, an open lineage, and a recovery lead are different things.

Current snapshot

Current official or dated evidence supports the track record at the stated access date.

Historical verified

Period, first-party, municipal, or event evidence supports the historical name or occurrence.

Open lineage

Predecessor, successor, rename, relocation, or site continuity remains unresolved.

Recovery open

Useful discovery evidence is retained below settled-public-history status until stronger corroboration is recovered.
