EVIDENCE STANDARDS

How sources, locations, dates, relationships, and media are handled.

The same standards are used across claims, locations, dates, relationships, and media.

01 · SOURCES

Claims link to their sources.

Each claim shows its source, confidence, and limits.

02 · LOCATIONS

Map points require geographic evidence.

Tracks without defensible coordinates remain unpinned rather than receiving a guessed city-center marker.

03 · TRACK RELATIONSHIPS

Similar names do not prove continuity.

Same city, related name, shared facility, or plausible chronology does not automatically establish a rename, predecessor/successor chain, ownership or organizational identity.

4 optional open historical connection questions remain explicit
04 · UNRESOLVED QUESTIONS

Open questions can stay open.

Stronger evidence can move one part of a record forward while exact site, operating span, historical connection or identity remains open. Some questions may remain unresolved.

05 · DATES

Dates are shown only as precisely as the source supports.

A source that establishes only a year or month is not presented as an exact day.

SOURCE RATINGS

Source strength and confidence are separate.

A strong source can still support only a narrow statement. A weaker or retrospective source can still be valuable when its limitation stays visible.

SOURCE CLASS
What kind of source is this?

Examples include official records, period publications, municipal documents, specialist sources, objects, and attributed memories.

CONFIDENCE
How strongly does this source support this specific claim?

Confidence applies to the specific statement being made — not to every possible conclusion someone could draw from the source.

LIMITS
What does this evidence not establish?

A strong source can still leave location, chronology, identity, or historical connection unresolved.

UNRESOLVED
Unknown is not the same as false.

An unresolved detail is simply not settled by the available evidence.

EXAMPLE

Sara Park BMX Raceway

Lake Havasu City · AZ-TRK-0111
Evidence foundA1 dated USA BMX occurrence

A 2013 USA BMX source establishes a dated Sara Park BMX Raceway occurrence in Lake Havasu City.

What it supportsDated history + physical SARA Park track site

The record can now publish the documented SARA Park physical track site while keeping identity and organization questions separate.

What remains openLondon Bridge / name / organizational continuity

The historical SARA point intentionally matches the current physical track site. That shared site does not prove London Bridge or later organizational/name continuity.
