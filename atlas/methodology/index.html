02 · GEOGRAPHIC ABSOLUTE

A dot is a geographic assertion.

Boundary and markers use one georeferenced Arizona frame. Registered latitude/longitude drives placement. Records without defensible coordinates do not receive substitute city-center pins.

03 · NO SILENT MERGES

Similarity is not continuity.

Same city, related name, shared facility, or plausible chronology does not automatically establish a rename, predecessor/successor chain, ownership or organizational identity.

04 · RECOVERY

Open questions can stay open.

Stronger evidence can move one part of a record forward while exact site, operating span, lineage or identity remains open. Recovery does not have to end in a clean answer.

05 · DATE PRECISION

The Timeline does not manufacture days.

HOW TO READ THE LABELS

Authority is not the same thing as certainty.

A strong source can still support only a narrow statement. A weaker or retrospective source can still be valuable when its limitation stays visible.

AUTHORITY
Who / what is the source?

The publisher, governing body, period publication, municipal record, specialist directory, object record or attributed witness context.

CONFIDENCE
How strongly does it control this bounded claim?

Confidence applies to the specific statement being made — not to every possible conclusion someone could draw from the source.

BOUNDARY
Where does the evidence stop?

This is the anti-overclaim field. It can preserve exact location, chronology, identity or lineage as OPEN even when another part of the record is strong.

OPEN
Unknown is not the same as false.

An unresolved field is a research state. It is not automatically a negative claim and it is not permission to invent a plausible answer.
