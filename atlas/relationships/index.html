GRAPH KEY

Connections show what the evidence supports — no more.

01Direction matters

The arrow follows the recorded relationship direction. It is not automatically a timeline or ownership chain.

02Confidence stays attached

HIGH, HIGH RETROSPECTIVE, MEDIUM-HIGH and control-only states are not visually flattened into one certainty level.

03Boundary is part of the citation

A relationship can be true at one scope while remaining insufficient to prove a broader story.

04Absence is not evidence

If two entities have no public edge here, the Atlas is not claiming they were unrelated. The edge simply has not been admitted.
