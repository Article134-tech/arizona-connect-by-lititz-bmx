# Arizona Connect v0.25 — Integrated Regroup Control

## Locked baseline

v0.25 treats the following as approved product layers:
- Arizona Connect launcher / Connection-family shell;
- Arizona BMX Atlas home;
- Geographic Absolute Arizona-only location system;
- 25-track profile family;
- Timeline evidence chronology;
- Relationships evidence graph;
- Recovery Desk;
- Atlas Methodology trust layer;
- Arizona Research Register and governed claim/source drilldown.

## Permitted v0.25 corrections

- current release/version text where stale;
- current README and deployment instructions;
- service-worker cache-key refresh;
- audit / QA / checksum documentation.

## Not permitted in this regroup

- redesigning approved screens;
- changing governed claims or evidence boundaries;
- changing registered coordinates;
- adding map markers;
- merging track identities or lineages;
- admitting new relationship edges;
- promoting recovery material;
- changing Hall of Fame selection status;
- copying third-party media.

## Geographic gate

Geographic Absolute remains permanent: only governed registered coordinates may generate points, and every point must remain inside the authoritative Arizona boundary in the shared projection.
